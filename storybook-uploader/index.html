Snuggle Up Storybooks Uploader

Snuggle Up Storybooks Uploader is a personal tool that its owner uses to publish their own narrated children's storybook videos to their own YouTube channel.

It signs in to that one channel with Google's permission to upload videos and set each video's title, description, tags, thumbnail and visibility. It is not offered to other people and does not access anyone else's account.
